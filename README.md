# Billion Universe

**Adapt to the person, not the platform.**

Billion Universe is a personal operating surface. Platforms ship one wireframe and ask a billion people to become the same user. This product inverts that: light, language, density, and gravity rearrange around who you are.

Your person model stays on this device. There is no account.

## The person model

Four human axes shape the entire interface:

| Axis | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Pace** | Spacing, type scale, and how many things sit in reach |
| **Gravity** | Which room you enter: Studio, Library, Bridge, or Hearth |
| **Climate** | Hour of the room — dawn, day, dusk, night |
| **Grain** | Voice and material — warm paper, sharp instrument, or soft cloth |

## Surfaces

- `/` — thesis
- `/arrive` — calibration that previews the universe as you choose
- `/universe` — the room that exists only for you
- `/witness` — inhabit Amina, Kenji, Noor, and Mateo to prove the same product becomes different worlds
